# Stage 12 — Deliverable: `final_report.md`

## Chosen audience

A **non-technical investment committee** — people who will act on the numbers but do not want to read code
or statistical derivations. They need decision-oriented headlines, plain-language assumptions and risks, and
a clear "what this means for you."

## Why this format fits

I chose a **single Markdown report** (rather than a slide deck or dashboard) because:

- **Text + charts reads linearly** — the audience can absorb the executive summary first, then drill into
  each chart, without clicking through slides or an app.
- **Self-contained** — the charts are embedded as exported PNGs (`images/`), so the report renders anywhere
  (GitHub, a wiki, or converted to PDF) with no runtime or hosting needed.
- **Easy to skim** — bullet takeaways under each chart give the decision-relevant interpretation at a glance,
  while the sensitivity table and tornado chart make the assumption-dependence explicit.
- **Reproducible** — the exact figures are regenerated by
  `homework12_results-reporting-delivery-design_submission.ipynb`, so a technical peer can re-run and audit
  every number.

## Files

- `final_report.md` → the stakeholder-facing deliverable
- `images/*.png` → exported figures (`risk_return.png`, `return_by_scenario.png`, `cumulative_return.png`,
  `tornado_assumptions.png`)
